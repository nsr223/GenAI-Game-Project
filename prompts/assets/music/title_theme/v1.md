# Title theme: v1

- **Tool:** `facebook/musicgen-small (transformers)`, run locally on CPU (236 s)
- **Settings:** 30 s, seed 1, guidance 3.0, sampling on
- **Status:** selected

## Prompt

```
Dark cinematic mystery theme for a murder investigation aboard a research ship in a winter storm. Slow solo piano motif in a minor key over low sustained cello and double bass, distant airy strings, sparse and atmospheric, 60 bpm, no drums.
```

## Result

Tonal and sparse: harmonic piano/string partials, phrases change roughly every 5 s, 79 % of the energy between 200 Hz and 1 kHz, no drum transients in the spectrogram. Fits a title screen.

**Post-processing:** 1 s fade-in, 3 s fade-out, RMS -20 dBFS → `frontend/static/audio/music/title_theme.ogg`.
