# Character Look Sheet (PROPOSED v1)

> **Status:** PROPOSED — open for team review. Nothing here is canon until the team agrees.
> **Owner:** Visuals (characters) · **Sources:** each character bible §1–2, Story §4, World §7.1.
> **Why this exists:** the character bibles define personality, voice and secrets in detail but say almost nothing about physical appearance. Image generators need fixed visual facts, otherwise every portrait drifts. This sheet is the single reference for how each person looks.

## Rules for every character

- **Setting:** a modern working research vessel in a December North Atlantic gale (World §1.1, §7.1). Practical, layered, slightly worn clothing — fleeces, work jackets, waterproofs. Nobody is dressed for an office or a yacht.
- **Everyone is tired.** The portraits show the morning after the discovery: subdued, sleep-deprived, but in control of their faces.
- **No one looks like a villain.** Maya in particular must read as warm and trustworthy (Maya §1, §2 "Avoid: smirking"). The art must not hint at the solution.
- **No spoilers in art:** no blood, no injuries, no reference mass or tools held like weapons (Frontend summary, World §3.2).
- **No real logos, company names or protected emblems.** In particular, Olivia must **not** wear a red cross (it is a legally protected emblem); use a plain "MEDIC" text-free patch or a green/white medical symbol without text.
- **Pose convention (all portraits):** waist-up, three-quarter view, body turned slightly toward the **right** of the frame (they stand on the left of the dialogue screen and face into the room, matching Erik's mockup).
- **Background for generation:** plain flat light-grey background so the figure can be cut out (see `style/v1.md`).
