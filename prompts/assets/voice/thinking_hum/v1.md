# Thinking hum: v1

A "Hmmmm" that plays while an NPC is thinking, i.e. while the UI shows "*Name* is thinking…" and waits for the LLM. It loops, so it lasts exactly as long as the model takes, and fades out when the reply arrives. A light comic touch for the waiting time; the accusation itself stays serious.

- **Tool:** `suno/bark-small` (transformers), run locally on CPU, about 45 s per take
- **Prompt (text to speak):** `Hmmmmmmmm...` with six takes: three voice presets for each voice type, two seeds for two of the presets
- **Selection rule:** the longest stretch of 50 ms frames that are loud (> -45 dBFS), strongly voiced (autocorrelation > 0.8) and in hum pitch range (70–350 Hz). A longer steady stretch gives a less mechanical loop. Spectrograms: [05-thinking-hum-takes](../../../../docs/prompt-log/audio/img/05-thinking-hum-takes.webp)

| male_a | `v2/en_speaker_6` | 1 | 3.7 s | 1.35 s (+ short gaps, about 2 s in total) | **Selected (male).** Pitch spread 2.7 %. |
| male_b | `v2/en_speaker_6` | 2 | 8.5 s | 0 s | Rejected: voiced but at noise-floor level (-74 dBFS). |
| male_c | `v2/en_speaker_3` | 1 | 2.4 s | 0 s | Rejected: breathy, almost no voiced hum. |
| female_a | `v2/en_speaker_9` | 1 | 3.7 s | 0.7 s | Rejected: hum too short to loop. |
| female_b | `v2/en_speaker_9` | 2 | 14.6 s | 0.6 s | Rejected: short hum, then 13 s of near-silence. |
| female_c | `v2/en_speaker_4` | 1 | 3.7 s | 2.85 s | **Selected (female).** Pitch spread 0.8 %, very steady. |

**Post-processing** (`docs/prompt-log/audio/humloop.py`): cut the steady part (male 1.45–3.25 s, female 1.0–3.6 s), crossfade the last 0.3 s into the start, RMS -20 dBFS. Result: `voice/thinking_male.ogg` (1.5 s loop) and `voice/thinking_female.ogg` (2.3 s loop). Measured over three repeats, the level jumps at most 1.2 dB at the loop point.

**In the game** (`frontend/static/audio.js`, settings in `audio.json` → `thinking`): starts 600 ms after "is thinking…" appears (so instant replies stay silent), loops, fades out over 0.3 s when the line disappears or the conversation closes. Sarah, Maya and Olivia use the female hum; Daniel, Thomas and Lucas the male one. Checked in headless Chrome with the fake LLM and a 3 s artificial delay: hum started 0.6 s in and stopped when the reply appeared (3.3 s).

**Ideas for v2:** one take per character instead of per voice type; a short closing "mm." when the reply arrives.
