# Editing room: v1

- **Tool:** `cvssp/audioldm-s-full-v2 (diffusers)`, run locally on CPU (128 s)
- **Settings:** 10 s, seed 1, 100 steps, guidance 2.5
- **Status:** selected

## Prompt

```
Steady low computer hum and quiet hard drive clicking in a small room
```

**Negative prompt:** `music, speech, silence, hiss, low quality`

## Result

Low hum (89 % below 200 Hz) with a few faint drive clicks, but also steady whine tones between 4 and 6 kHz.

**Post-processing:** 3 kHz low-pass to remove the whine, seamless loop, RMS -34 dBFS → `ambience/editing_room.ogg`.
