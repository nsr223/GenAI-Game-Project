# Crew quarters: v1

- **Tool:** `cvssp/audioldm-s-full-v2 (diffusers)`, run locally on CPU (132 s)
- **Settings:** 10 s, seed 1, 100 steps, guidance 2.5
- **Status:** selected

## Prompt

```
Rain against a small porthole and the low creaking of a ship's hull inside a cabin
```

**Negative prompt:** `music, speech, low quality`

## Result

Rain-like ticks with low creaks, energy spread across low and mid bands. Used for the crew quarters and all eight cabins.

**Post-processing:** Seamless loop, RMS -30 dBFS → `ambience/crew_quarters.ogg`.
