# Dry lab: v2

- **Tool:** `cvssp/audioldm-s-full-v2 (diffusers)`, run locally on CPU (105 s)
- **Settings:** 10 s, seed 1, 100 steps, guidance 2.5
- **Status:** selected

## Prompt

```
Computer fans humming steadily in a small room with occasional keyboard typing
```

**Negative prompt:** `music, speech, silence, low quality`

## Result

Audible now, but a bright fan hiss (80 % of the energy between 1 and 4 kHz). Change: removed 'quiet'/'soft', described a steady fan, negative 'silence'. Kept and corrected in post-processing instead of a third generation, since a fan heard inside a closed computer case is duller than this.

**Post-processing:** 2.5 kHz low-pass filter, seamless loop, RMS -34 dBFS (the quietest room, as the bible describes the dry lab) → `ambience/dry_lab.ogg`.
