Fine, irregular carbon-ink contours, short directional hatching, translucent watercolour pooling on pale cold-pressed paper. Leave paper white visible through broken washes and along lit edges. Finish with clear selective linework and broad, economical shapes.

Draw a PROPOSED cast study for a contemporary North Atlantic research vessel. One landscape sheet with six separate waist-up cutouts in a three-column, two-row grid, reading across. Generous empty warm-white paper surrounds EACH figure, including below the waist and between cells. No text, frame lines, furniture, environment or wash behind them. Six different faces with natural proportions and lived-in asymmetry. Low-detail observational drawing, just enough marks to catch the individual. Ordinary conversation, neutral and unguarded. Vary the directions of the faces, the weight of the torsos and the shapes of the empty spaces around elbows. Keep every hand and hair tip on the page.
