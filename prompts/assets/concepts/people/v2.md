Fine, irregular carbon-ink contours, short directional hatching, translucent watercolour pooling on pale cold-pressed paper. Leave paper white visible through broken washes and along lit edges. Finish with clear selective linework and broad, economical shapes.

Draw a PROPOSED cast study for a contemporary North Atlantic research vessel. One landscape sheet with six separate waist-up cutouts in a three-column, two-row grid, reading across. Generous empty warm-white paper surrounds EACH figure, including below the waist and between cells. No text, frame lines, furniture, environment or wash behind them. Six different faces with natural proportions and lived-in asymmetry. Draw smaller on the sheet so each entire cutout has a paper margin of at least a thumb width. Simplify each figure into a few thin washes with visible clear pen lines for the features, hardly any marks in fabric; the untouched paper does much of the drawing. Use unmistakably individual local light on each form: divide each face into its own distinct light and shadow planes; do not illuminate the ensemble from a studio window. Ordinary conversation, neutral and unguarded. Vary the directions of the faces, the weight of the torsos and the shapes of the empty spaces around elbows. Keep every hand and hair tip on the page.

Top left, Thomas Blake. Compact and thick through the middle, in his fifties. Full cheeks, small chin, neat black hair with a high side part. Pressed pale shirt beneath a fitted knitted vest, one frayed mustard cuff. Seen in three-quarter view from high on his right shoulder, leaning back to his left. Holds up two fingers of one hand at chest height, touches one with the other hand as he counts; gaze thoughtful, level to his right. Draw the planes facing down and right in warm light; the forehead is in a soft cool shadow.
Top centre, Sarah Morgan. Tall long column of a woman, lined angular face, long dark braid visible down the back of her navy duty shirt. Narrow side profile from slightly below, chin very slightly raised to turn one ear towards the listener; eyes steady at the horizon. One arm long by her side; the other thumb rubs the shirt's side seam low on the hip. Salt-grey stitching. Cool daylight from behind picks out the braid and ear. Leave a paper-white rim around her dark profile; the far cheek disappears into a blue wash.
Top right, Maya Chen. Medium build, rounded cheeks and small rounded nose, straight blunt black fringe, hair tied in a low ponytail. Open canvas overshirt over a rust henley. A forward diagonal from hips to head. Seen a little from above, eyes following her index finger tracing a diagram in the air low before her; other index finger taps its endpoint. Two low, open elbows; a diffuse vertical skylight falls on her crown and forearms; bangs cast a narrow shadow down over both brows.
Bottom left, Daniel Reyes. Lean and sinewy, late fifties, long clean-shaven jaw, heavy brow, short receding salt-and-pepper crop. Grease-softened ochre canvas work vest. Side-on facing right, curled into an arch over a small blunt pipe coupling, eyes almost closed as both hands find its seam by touch. Hands close and gentle, forearms low. Cold steel throws light up onto the underside of his nose and fingertips; a single grey wash shades the brow. Pull his whole bent back and elbows inside the panel.
Bottom centre, Olivia Hart. Broad soft shoulders, round full face, dark brown skin, close short twists. Plum cardigan. Near-frontal seated upright, chin level; loosely crossed forearms make a low horizontal oval across her waist; hands rest open on opposite sleeves. Calm direct gaze. Low warm window light catches her left cheek only; draw the opposite side as a broad unified umber shape.
Bottom right, Lucas Weber. Compact, wiry, in his twenties; slim tapering chin, uneven crooked smile, straight black fringe cut above his brows. Green overshirt with mended cuffs. Hips twisting away to the right; looks back, one elbow high, thumb and forefinger form a motionless L at eye height to compose an imaginary frame; other hand low. Cool light from a high slit on the right cuts diagonally across his temple and raised fingers; let the far shoulder fade. His small smile is easy, eyes open and interested.
