# Task Summary: Visual and Audio Assets

> Read `canon.md` first. Bibles win on conflict.
> Deep dives: World §7 (atmosphere), World §3 (room contents), character §1–2 (appearance and personality).

## Visual direction (World §7.1)
- **Modern working research vessel**: not a luxury yacht, warship or sci-fi ship (World §1.1).
- Metal bulkheads, anti-slip floors, utilitarian doors, sealed cabinets, bolted furniture.
- Muted grey/blue in technical rooms; warmer light in the mess hall and living spaces.
- Contemporary instruments and ordinary computers: **no holograms or futuristic UI**.
- Used but maintained: cables, labels, protective gear, handwritten notes.
- Tight rooms. Outside: a mid-December North Atlantic **heavy gale**, at night or grey morning.

## Room details that must be right
- **Wet lab** (crime scene): stainless benches, pumps, sample containers, an automated **sampling apparatus with a broad hinged metal service cover**, and a **calibration station** holding a squat cylindrical steel reference mass. Humid, with machinery noise. **Don't draw the body or make the mass look like a weapon** in default art.
- **Dry lab**: quiet, climate-controlled, monitors and data screens.
- **Bridge**: navigation screens, radar, comms gear, storm visible through windows.
- **Editing room**: a converted survey office with laptop, monitors, cameras and drives.
- **Engineering workshop**: tools, spare parts, maintenance terminal; loud and utilitarian.

## Audio direction (World §7.2)
- Constant low engine, generator, ventilation or hull noise.
- Rain, wind and waves near windows and exterior spaces.
- Per-room character: pump hum (wet lab), keyboard and fans (dry lab), tools (workshop), radio bursts (bridge).
- Dialogue must stay audible; ambience supports tension.
- **Weather never calms** during the investigation (World §7.3).
- Voices (TTS) are optional: match each character's speaking style in their bible §2.
